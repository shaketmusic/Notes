Whenever you use a device ask yourself – should we do serial processing or parallel?

### Serial/insert
we are adding effects to the affected sound. Putting eq and a compressor – the compressor will compress the EQ’d sound.

### Parallel/Send 
we create another auxiliary track, sending the original audio to that new chain and mixing them together. We have a dry sound and effected signal independently. It’s good for more control (process only the affected signal) and more management (we can have one reverb for all the vocals, or the drums and just change one thing).

In Ableton you can group your audio effects to an audio effect rack (Cmnd G), open the chain tab, right click next to your existing chain and select **create chain**. 

![[Ableton Create Chain.png|226]]

In ProTools, you can **send to new track** instead of picking a bud and assigning sends to it. That make the process easier and faster. 

**The Abby Road Trick** – EQ your reverb! Before the reverb drop an EQ and roll off all the low end (because it creates ramble) and the highs. See also [[Reverb Hygiene]].

**EQ and Dynamics** – typically inserts. EQ cuts => Compression => EQ boosts

**Delay & Reverb** – typically sends
