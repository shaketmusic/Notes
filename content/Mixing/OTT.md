OTT is a preset in Ableton for the multiband compressor that does both downwards and upwards compression/expansion. It really works wonders on reverb, arpeggiated synths. Essential in EDM.

The key is the Amount knob. It’s not a dry wet, it’s scaling down the ratios of the compression. If you don’t want that EDM sound, 20%-30% sound really good as well. 

The other important know is the Time knob – it slows down the attack and release (with the time fully up it will make a sound tight and clear) if turn it down we get no transient and all sustain.


### Tips

- It’s not great for Sub Base, though. 

- OTT is not a fix for everything. You need to mix before it.

- OTT is just a preset. You can make your own custom OTT

- You can use Multiband Compressor to remove reverb or add more reverb – set your threshold for the quite stuff and decide whether to expand or compress it. Same thing with the loud stuff.
