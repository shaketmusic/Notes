


### Headphones: Open - Back vs closed back
Open back headphones are more recommended for mixing, because they tend to sound more natural.

Closed back headphones are more suitable for recording.
**Headphone recommendation:** 
- Sennheiser HD 650 Open – Back, kind of industry standard.
- MASSDROP Sennheiser HD 6XX, recommended alternative. Almost the Same, less then half the price.

### Speakers
When using speakers, you are hearing not the actual speakers but also your room. Every room is different. Every room emphasises and masks different frequencies. Rooms can be treated with acoustic absorptions, diffusers and base traps. It's very expensive but with not a lot of effort you can build pretty good acoustic panels for cheap. I'm not an acoustics expert so you'll have to do the research yourself. Just know that every room is different and requires different treatment.

However, there are a few things you can do right now to improve how you hear from the speakers.

1. Pick a rectangle space. **Never work in a circular space**
2. Your listening position should be in front of the center of the short wall, set back 37.5% of the room length.
3. The speakers need to be freestanding or flush mounted, your desk should be as small as possible
4. **The median Plane** - Whatever distance the speakers are from themselves – you want to be at the same distance. If you’re not at the same distance your stereo image wouldn’t be accurate.
