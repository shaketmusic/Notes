
 
- Compress/ saturate your delays and reverbs to glue them together

- When you do Sidechain compression to a kick, filter the lows of the kick to have better control of the ducking.

- Use shaper box side chain volume in multiband – really compress the lows but the highs have a really fast attack so the kick cuts through, but you don’t get ducking. Also draw the shape of the kick and reverse it.

- After mixing, go through every track in the session and check to make sure everything is fine for the whole duration of the song, and fix inconsistencies if need be. Is this clap volume always consistent? The Vocals usually are the biggest concern.

- **Utility automation**: The goal with automation is to make sure it doesn’t sound like automation.

- Sometime its good to boost the instrumental when the vocals aren’t singing, make it sound like the band is stepping behind the vocals and moving forward when they have the space. **But make it invisible. People don’t need to know you did it.**

- **Drama automation** - Automations are really good for transitions, extenuations. Adding shapes.

- **Don’t send anything to a client if you’re not ok with releasing it**.
