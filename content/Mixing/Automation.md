Automation is a way to have parameters change over time. You can automate either in real time or non-real time (drawing automation). In ProTools – hold command and click break point that you can then move. To delete use option + click. 

- If you aoutomate volume use ProTools' **Trim** or Ableton's **Utility.**

In Pro Tools, use **Touch** or **Latch** modes (if you use **write** mode it could override other automation. **Don’t.**)

### Why to Automate

1. Fix errors (a part gets quite/loud in the middle of the song). This is utilitarian: we are fixing problems. **Almost every mix needs a little automation**.

2. Drama automation: adding automation to create more drama: Turning the bass a little before the chorus to make the chorus more impactful. Or even turning the whole mix of the buildup, adding reverb at the end of the chorus....

3. Ear candy – push up sounds a little when the vocals are not singing etc.
