**Dynamics in music**: how loud can a source be.
**Dynamics in mixing:** what is the range between the loud and the soft.

There are two families of dynamic processors: **Compressors** and **expanders**. There are two types of compressors and expanders – either **downward** (make the louder stuff quieter) and **upward** (take the low stuff and make them louder).
