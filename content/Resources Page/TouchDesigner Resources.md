## Use iPhone as an Input Camera

iPhones provide a featured called **Continuity Camera**, which allows the computer to use the iPhone’s camera as a device over a wireless network.

1. In your iPhone settings, enable **Continuity Camera**.
    
    > Go to `Settings > General > AirPlay & Continuity`
    > 
2. Make sure both your iPhone and mac are connected to the same iCloud account.
3. Both devices must have WiFi and Bluetooth enabled.
    
    > Although the Continuity Camera works wirelessly, you may also use a USB cable.
    > 
4. You should now be able to access your iPhone camera as long as it is locked.
