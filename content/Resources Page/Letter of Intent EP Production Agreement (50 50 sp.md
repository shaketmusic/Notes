### 1. Scope of Work & Schedule

The Producer agrees to provide production, engineering, and mixing services for a total of **[Number, e.g., 4 or 5]** tracks. To ensure steady progress, both parties commit to the following weekly cadence:

- **Weekly Production Meeting:** A recurring sync-up (in-person or remote) once per week to review progress and set goals.
- **Individual "Deep Work" Sessions:** In addition to the meeting, both the Artist and Producer commit to **at least one focused solo session per week** dedicated to the Project (e.g., Artist recording vocal demos/writing; Producer fleshing out arrangements/sound design).

### 2. Compensation & Ownership

In lieu of an upfront production fee, the parties agree to the following revenue and ownership splits:

- **Master Recording:** Producer shall own 50% of the Master Sound Recording.
- **Publishing (Composition):** Producer shall be credited as a co-writer and own **33.3%** of the underlying composition (Music/Lyrics), including all associated **Mechanical and Performance royalties**.
- **Automatic Revenue Splits:** Artist agrees to set up automated "split-pay" via their digital distributor (e.g., DistroKid, TuneCore) so that the Producer’s share is paid directly to the Producer upon release.

### 3. Defined Revision Policy

To protect the Project timeline, "Revisions" are defined and limited as follows:

- **The "Consolidated List" Rule:** A Revision is defined as **one single, comprehensive list of feedback** (arrangement, production, or mix notes) sent by the Artist. The Producer will address the entire list in one pass to create the next version.
- **Revision Limit:** Producer provides **two (2) included Mix Revisions** per track.
- **Production Lock:** Once the instrumental arrangement is "locked" and vocal mixing begins, any subsequent requests for structural changes (e.g., changing the tempo or song structure) will count as an additional Revision.
- **Technical Fixes:** Corrections for technical errors (e.g., audio pops, accidental mutes) do not count against the revision limit.

### 4. Deliverables & Expectations

- **Artist Readiness:** Artist agrees to arrive at all sessions rehearsed. Final vocal takes must be completed before the final mixing phase begins.
- **Final Assets:** Upon completion and approval, Producer will deliver the final mastered WAV files, Instrumental, Acapela, and Stems (Drums, Bass, Keys, Guitars, FX, Back Vox).

### 5. Expenses

Any out-of-pocket costs (e.g., external studio rental, session musicians, or professional mastering) are the responsibility of the **Artist** and must be approved in writing by both parties before being incurred.

### 6. Termination

If the Artist decides to move the project to another producer or elects not to release the Project, the Artist agrees to pay the Producer a flat "time-equity" fee of $500 per song, or the Producer retains 100% ownership of all unfinished recordings created during these sessions.
