### “Tape Loops” in Ableton

Use when need a texture. Really cool with synths, guitars, even vocals.

1. Put the Looper Device on a return track (You can map a button to the Looper)
2. Record a couple seconds of silence.
3. Play loops randomly, some of them fade in to the looper send, some of them allow the attack to come in.
4. Drug the audio loop to the arrangement and you’ll have a new texture to use
5. To make it ambient – stretch it, put different layers with different splits.
6. Maybe bounce the result and put into a drum rack to resample

Optional Listen: William Besinsky - The Disintegration Loops

### Recording at the wrong speed

1. Decide on a number to transpose to.
2. Figure how much to speed the tempo to transpose it up by the same amount
3. Pitch up the midi and make the tempo the faster tempo
4. Pitch down using re-pitch

You can do that with audio, solo track or with whole songs. It’s common in Hip Hop and Hyper Pop.

### “Bad Edits”
Sometimes bad edits can help the groove and impact. Try cutting the last 32nd or 64th audio slice at the end of the loop.

### Glitch
Take any song.

1. Copy the song, paste to the new track, load to the sampler and chop it to half notes.
2. Make the song really slow with warping. Use gate mode.
3. Put a groove with the slices. Leave some gaps.
4. Transpose down
5. OTT and Drum Buss

Put the warping to texture mode and experiment with grain size
