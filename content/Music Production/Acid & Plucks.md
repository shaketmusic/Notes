### Acid Bass
A plucky bass sound originally from the Roland tb-303. It’s characterized by its squelchy filter.

**Tips**
- Make The Envelope open the Filter **but also the resonance**
- Put velocity on both cutoff and resonance.
- Put the velocity on the envelope decay time.
- Add the **pedal** Ableton effect with the distort option (originally they used a tube screamer to distort.
- Add a short reverb

### Plucks
The traditional pluck uses one envelope both on the volume and filter. Add a little bit of delay and reverb, and multiband compression on everything.

For a more modern pluck, You can use two envelopes for the filter and volume independently. The volume will gate and only the filter will decay. That way we get more bass.

Neither is better, it’s an arrangement thing – do we need the bass or do we have other bass parts?

In any case, Instead of modulating only the filter cuttof, **modulate both the filter and the filter Env decay** using a macro.
