
Every analog sound has distortion. So to make the production sound more real we need to add distortion.

- If we build a synth from sine waves and add harmonics with distortion, we get a more interesting sound that responds to dynamics. Like how real instruments are brighter when you play them live.

- Another way of building tension instead of doing a filter sweep, we can use distortion (like adjusting the release so that the overlapping notes get distorted – intermodulation distortion)

- A distorted sine wave pluck is a more unique pluck then a filtered saw plucked

**For Bass House:**

1. You take two sine waves with a non-pure interval between them. We distort the sound and introduce the higher sine to get a Habstrakt style Bass.

2. You can then put two LFOs on the two sine levels. You can also put macros on the second sine’s course and fine tune to have variation of the sound.

3. Modulate the LFO shape to add variation.
