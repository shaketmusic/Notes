We will use Ableton's **Operator** to create the snare, but this advice applies to almost every synthesizer.


1. Turn velocity and filter off.
2. Turn fixed mode on the oscillator and tune the snare (200-500hz)
3. Adjust the decay to around 180ms.
4. Add a little of pitch envelope.
5. Go to additive mode and add 3ms of noise. That’s our stick.
6. Add another noise oscillator, turn the peack and initial all the way down, so we get a delay. Do a 10-20 decay.
7. **Making the reverb – crate a noise burst and use multiband dynamic to upward compress only the mids so they stay longer then the gihs.**
8. Add some foley hit
9. Compress just the sustain to have a louder transient
10. Normalize
11. Add a short reverb. Mix a little bit of it just after the attack
