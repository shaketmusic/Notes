
When we need a different sound for the song, it’s more efficient and musically coherent to take the original sound, copy it and change it a little. The two sounds are related, and are an evolution of one another.

- You can change FM amounts, LFO or envelope shapes, LFO rates, Ranges, oscillator mixes etc.

- Keep duplicating and mutating the sounds. You may find something really cool further down the line.

- Group the sounds together and process them as a group (OTT, reverb, Multiband Compression, Saturator, side chain). That way they all feel like they are from the same place.

- You can use it to make more interesting chords: Have four variations and have each of them play one voice of a chord underneath a simple pad with reverb.
