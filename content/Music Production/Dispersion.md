
Like prism, but for sound. All frequencies come at the same time to the input and then go out at different times. We can do this by stacking a lot of cheap EQ’s that are doing phase shoft, like EQ3. **Bass producers put that on their basses.**

![[Dispersion 1.png]]

We can build that with multiband dynamic group. We map the high and low split frequencies to macros, then have three layes with solo’d Highs, Mids and Lows. Now we can process those separately.

![[Dispersion 2.png]]

We then use align delay to delay the lows by 12 ms and the meds by 6 ms. Now the attack is only the highs, followed by the meds and the lows.
