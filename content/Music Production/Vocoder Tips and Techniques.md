### Stadium effect 
white noise with 1000k boost kind of sounds like crowd screaming and clapping.

1. take a vocoder with noise carrier
2. crank the attack and release longer
3. boost at 1000k
4. remove the highs at around 8k
5. add reverb
6. multiband compression
7. mix in a crowd sample
8. record 1-2 takes of real gang vocal and mix that in

### Vocode drum loops
Put a vocoder on a drum loop. It’s really cool with a noise vocoder. You can mix that in with the real drums. Alternatively use it with a synth as the cariier and to have harmonic pitch information.

### Vocode textures
Why not use nature sounds, or textures/foley/ambience? Everything that has a cool rhythm or texture is good for vocoding.

### Sine wave as a vocoder modulator
A sine wave as a modulator is only amplifying the fundamental – effectively creating a bandpass filter. So if we manipulate these sine wave we can use the vocoder as a super flexible-configurable filter.

- What if we randomize the sine wave pitch?
- What if we add another random sine voice?
- What if we unison detune 16 sine waves 4 octaves apart? And modulated the pitch with an LFO? What if we use 32 oscillators? What if we put an LFP on the volume of those sine waves?

### Set the modulator as the carrier
Then put a few random harmonics, and distort the whole thing. This is a great way to make growls. If you turn the bend width down it almost sounds like a guitar feedback.

### Pitch tracking
This mode tracks the pitch of the modulator, generating a waveform the plays as the carrier. Maybe could be useful some day?

### Noise cleaner
