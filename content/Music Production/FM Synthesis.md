
FM essentially is audio rate vibrato. We can describe why this phenomenon happens using Bessel functions.

**What does it mean** – when we do FM, we’re adding side bans higher and lower to the original. Because of that, sometimes the fundamental gets attenuated because some co sines are created and they interfere with the harmonics. The frequency of the wave doing the FM will influence what frequencies are getting added/lowered. We get A + B and A – B harmonics.

While in subtractive synthesis harmonics get added and removed, with FM harmonics always move as you change the amount.

FM is also very good for metallic sounds, because we can use Frequencies that aren’t the fundamental’s harmonics.

In Serum, to create the wubbi donk Tech House sound, use PD (phase distortion) and not FM. The PD keeps the fundamental and the sweeps are more musical. Operator is also working with Phase Distortion.
