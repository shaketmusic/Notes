
Key tracking is often overlooked but it can help with making sounds feel more real, because most instruments maintain their harmonics over the range.

- You can use noise and comb filters (even straight resonance) with key tracking for out of the box bass.

- The Sandstorm sound: Slam a key-tracked bandpass filter with resonance into a distortion.

- You don't have to only key track the filter. What if we use key tracking on a polyphonic LFO to control its speed?
