### Stealing Kicks

1. Chop the kick from a song.
2. Automate the EQ so the highs are only at the very beginning. That way we get the transient too.

### Improving Kicks

1. Take an acoustic kick
2. Add a lowpass filter with the EQ and Raise the resonance of the bass. That gives us lowend.

![[Improving Kick 2.png]]

3. Grab shifter and shift it 10-15hz.

![[Improving Kicks 3.png]]

4. Boost the Highs but only on the couple ms of the attack

![[Improving Kicks 4.png]]

5. Cut the low end only after the attack stage to remove the low end rumble.

You can also splice kicks together – take the attack stage of one kick and put the body stage of another.
