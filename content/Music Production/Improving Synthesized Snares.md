**Improving Synthesized Snare**

1. High Pass x4 and boost the resonance at the fundamental

![[Improving Snares 1.png]]

2. EQ the highs out shortly after the attack with a high shelf

![[Improving Snares 2.png]]

3. Add another EQ, Start from boosting a lot, immediately drop down to zero and bring back up.

![[Improving Snares 3.png]]

4. Boost at around 1-2k

![[Improving Snares 4.png]]

5. Eq out the lows after the initial attack
6. Overdrive to taste
7. Remove the rumble with a HighPass Filter
8. Compress

![[Improving Snares 8.png]]

9. Clip
