The basic formula for a kick is a sine wave with a really fast pitch envelope. We will use Ableton's **Operator** to create the kicks, but this advice applies to almost every synthesizer.

### Kicks
1. Turn velocity and filter off.
2. Turn fixed mode on the oscillator and tune the first one to 50Hz.
3. Set the envelope to match your reference/to taste
4. Turn on Pitch Envelope. Make the initial +38 and then end -48. Now, because the end of the envelope will play 4 octaves bellow, we raise the oscillator 4 octaves – to 800hz.
5. Old school kicks have a noise on the attack to get that little bit of transient. Modern kicks we FM the attack of the first oscillator with the noise oscillator
6. We use an EQ to cut around 200hz to separate the transient and body of the kick.
7.  Add a bost at 90 and at the highs
8. Saturate a little
9. Normalize – hard clip and raise the volume until it just clips.

### Rumble Kick

1. Take a kick
2. Add reverb
3. Add distortion
4. Filter
5. You can put a delay to make it gallop
6. Record to audio
7. Side chain to your actual kick
