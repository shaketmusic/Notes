
### Practice

- One thing that gets neglected a lot of the time by producers is sound design practice. Make sure you put time to do sound design and preset making sessions. It will be invaluable in session and also will make you better at those things.

- You can’t become better at everything all at once. Take a week and improve in one thing specific: chords, drums, sound design, fast workflow, guitar etc.

- At the end of the day, most of the music we make is for vocals. To make sure we don't over produce, work on producing beats with acapellas, and remove them later (just be careful not to fall in love with the vocal – don’t put it on loop. Also, never play the vocals for your Topliner).

- **Challange yourself to produce with restrictions:**
	- Use only one sample as your sound source; 
	- Produce a full song in an hour; 
	- Use only sine waves and Distortions; 
	- Work only with samples, or with only one synth; 
	- Make a song with one chord
	- Make a song in an odd time signature
	- Use plugins you never got to learn
	- Produce a song without any reverb
	- Produce a full track with only 4 or 8 tracks, like they did in the 60's-70's.
	- Learn how a record was made and produce a song with the same methods


### Workflow

- If an idea is not working within 15 minutes, it’s not gonna work.

- Practice making beats fast every day. Your ability to come up with good ideas fast and under pressure is one of the most valuables skills you can have as a producer.

- When trying to learn from others, don’t try to recreate just one sound. A lot of the times sounds make so much more sense in a context of a full produciton. They are working not only because of how they sound, but also because everything else compliments them. Try to do a sound alike production and you’ll learn a lot. 

- When you teach someone you get a lot better. Find someone starting out and help them. It's what this site is all about, so if you found anything valuable here pass it along.
