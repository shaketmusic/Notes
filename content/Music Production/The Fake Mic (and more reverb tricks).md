### The fake mic

We’re trying to emulate moving the mic away on a synth track.

1. Add low cut, pre delay to zero.
2. Make the size 1-3
3. Decay less than 300ms

All other processes (even reverbs) should come after. It really blends the synth in the track and puts it in space.


### Reverb on untuned vocals

Untuned vocals or sources can make unpleasant, beating reverb. Th solution: put Auto Tune on the reverb chain and it makes just the reverb cleaner.


### End of Phrase Reverb

A lot of EDM producers have a very long reverb chain that they automate in (mix is on zero and being automated) at the end of the phrase to create this reverse effect.
