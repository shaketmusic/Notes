We will use Ableton's **Operator** to create the claps and hats, but this advice applies to almost every synthesizer.


### Making Claps

1. For claps we need 4 noise bursts around 5-10ms appart. The last one can be longer
2. Add a High Pass filter with resonance
3. Overdrive
4. EQ – boost at 1000k.
5. Normalize
6. Tip – the time knob on operator can help make more tight or loose claps



### Hi Hat

1. Short bursts of noise
2. High pas filter
3. Attach an envelope for the filter and velocity to the length of the envelope
