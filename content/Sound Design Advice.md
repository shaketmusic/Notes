### Effects
- When You’re Producing, sometimes it’s cool to add reverbs and delays as inserts.
- Why not compress after the reverb?
- Put reverb on your bass before your compression and distortion
- Instead of chorus, try using a **frequency shifter** slightly shifted with 50% mix.
- Ableton’s shifter has a delay that is cool for pitch shifting feedback delays.



### Variation and Modulation
- Use Synth Macros mapped to different things and use it with Ableton’s modulation to create variations.
- Ableton’s automation doesn’t handle instant changes very well, so it’s better to curve it.
- Where an amateur sound designer will modulate one parameter, a good sound designer will modulate two.
- Create multiple different sounds and move through them with the same melody. You can put different Arps on some of the sounds to have ratchets.
- Too wide modulation can make sounds feel immature. Focus on subtle, compounding modulation.


### Workflow
- If your downbeat has a kick, bass and pluck – try moving some of them a couple ms. That makes everything feels more alive and gives more headroom.

- Make “sample packs” of you noodling with sound design (for each chord of your arrangement), then pick and choose the best takes as a collage. If you rename the track between each chord the file names would make sense. Look for moments using SHIFT+Drag

- Gain + Level compensator trick: Take two utilities, group together and map their gain to the same macro. Invert one of them and put the OTT/Drive/Saturator in the middle.
